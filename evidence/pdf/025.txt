edge-analytics counts any side that is not exactly “bid”/“buy” as a sell, so “BUY” or “long” flips the edge verdict

Severity: normal
Area: app

 Steps to reproduce

   1. cd tools/edge-analytics
   2. Make a one-fill trade log in the documented csv-logger shape and a mid log (files in evidence/
      candidates/kit20-23/ea/ ): t_buy.csv / t_BUY.csv / t_long.csv :
      1730000000,hideki,P,<side>,fill,1,100,1,100,0x, ; m2.csv : mids 100.05 at t0 and t0+1s, 100.30 at
      t0+10s, 100.50 at t0+60s.
   3. npx tsx src/index.ts --trades t_<side>.csv --mid m2.csv for each side.

 Expected

BUY and buy give the same report. A side the tool does not recognise ( long , short , blank, a typo) is
rejected or at least warned about, not silently read as a sell. The README lists only bid/buy and ask/sell.
Perp bots in this same kit name sides long / short ( packages/perp-core/src/orders.ts:28 ).

 Actual

 == side=buy    captured spread: median 5.00 bps | 60s net +50.0 bps | VERDICT: Positive marked-to-mid edge
 == side=BUY    captured spread: median -5.00 bps | 60s net -50.0 bps | VERDICT: NEGATIVE EDGE ... change
 the edge.
 == side=long   captured spread: median -5.00 bps | 60s net -50.0 bps | VERDICT: NEGATIVE EDGE ... change
 the edge.


toFillSide is s === "bid" || s === "buy" ? "buy" : "sell" . It is case-sensitive and has no error branch,
so every unknown value becomes a sell, every sign flips, and a profitable maker is told it has negative
edge (and the reverse) with no warning.
