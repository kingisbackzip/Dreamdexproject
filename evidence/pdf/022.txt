perp-starter: any PERP_SIDE other than “short” (e.g. “sell”, “bear”) silently opens a LONG

Severity: major
Area: SDK

 Steps to reproduce

   1. cd strategies/perp-starter
   2. NETWORK=hideki PERP_SYMBOL=BTC-PERP PERP_SIDE=sell npx tsx src/index.ts
   3. Repeat with PERP_SIDE=bear and PERP_SIDE=short .

 Expected

An unrecognised value is rejected at startup (the kit already throws on non-numeric values via envNum ,
and on a bad NETWORK / OWNER_ADDRESS). It should never be mapped to the opposite direction.

 Actual

PERP_SIDE=sell (the spot/ccxt word for going short) and PERP_SIDE=bear both give … | long 50 USDso at
2x … and [dry-run] would place open long 0.00064 BTC @ 77592.1 . Only short (any case/whitespace) goes
short. With DRY_RUN=false the bot would open a leveraged position in the opposite direction from the
one intended, and arm a bracket around it. Cause: strategies/perp-starter/src/index.ts:57 :
(process.env.PERP_SIDE ?? "long").trim().toLowerCase() === "short" ? "short" : "long" .


 Evidence

on Hideki:

 true

 == PERP_SIDE='SHORT'

strategies/perp-starter/src/index.ts:57 ; README.md:11 documents only long / short , with no warning
