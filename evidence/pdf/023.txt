perp-starter accepts negative or >=100 PERP_TAKE_PROFIT_PCT / PERP_STOP_LOSS_PCT and arms an inverted or negative-price bracket

Severity: normal
Area: SDK

 Steps to reproduce

   1. cd strategies/perp-starter
   2. NETWORK=hideki PERP_SYMBOL=BTC-PERP PERP_TAKE_PROFIT_PCT=-2 PERP_STOP_LOSS_PCT=-1 npx tsx src/
      index.ts
   3. NETWORK=hideki PERP_SYMBOL=BTC-PERP PERP_STOP_LOSS_PCT=150 npx tsx src/index.ts

 Expected

Percentages must be > 0 (and a stop-loss < 100), or startup fails with a clear message, the way envNum
already rejects non-numbers.

 Actual

    • Step 2: for a LONG at ~77,586 the “take-profit” is armed at 75,704.70 (below entry) and the
      “stop-loss” at 78,022.20 (above entry). Both triggers sit on the wrong side, so the take-profit
      closes at a loss and the stop-loss fires on a gain. No warning.
    • Step 3: stop-loss -38624.80 , a negative trigger price, is passed on as the bracket’s stop. Cause:
      envNum ( packages/perp-core/src/config.ts:55-63 ) only checks Number.isFinite . strategies/perp-
      starter/src/index.ts:174-177 computes mark*(1±pct/100) without checking sign or range. A sign
      mistake is easy because the README talks about a stop-loss “of 1%” as a loss amount, and some
      UIs take the stop as a negative %.
