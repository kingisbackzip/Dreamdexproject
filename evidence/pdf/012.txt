Unified perp market and funding structs break ccxt semantics that EXCHANGE.md promises: no contractSize/linear/swap on markets, and fundingRate is per 8h

Severity: normal
Area: SDK

 Steps to reproduce

   1. Run scripts/s22-ccxt-field-semantics.mjs (read-only).
   2. Inspect exchange.markets["ETH/USDso:USDso"] , exchange.fetchTicker("ETH/USDso:USDso") and
       exchange.fetchFundingRateHistory(...) .


 Expected

docs/EXCHANGE.md:5-6: “if you’ve driven a venue through ccxt, nothing here will surprise you, down to
the field names”. In ccxt’s unified structures a swap market carries swap / spot , linear / inverse and
contractSize ; ticker.percentage is in percent (−0.37 means −0.37%); fundingRate is the rate charged
per funding interval.

 Actual

    • The perp market struct has only id, chainId, symbol, type, base, quote, settle, active,
      contract, precision, limits, indexed, perpStatus, info . All 21 perp markets lack contractSize ,
       linear , swap , spot , inverse , taker / maker . The standard ccxt notional
       amount * market.contractSize * price gives NaN .
    • ticker.fundingRate and every fetchFundingRateHistory row are per 8 hours (dist/unified/
      structs.d.ts:446), while every live pool settles each 3600 s; a ccxt carry/arb calculation that treats
      it as per-interval overstates funding ×8. These conventions are stated only in the struct JSDoc;
      EXCHANGE.md, the page that makes the ccxt promise, never lists them.

Scope: the market struct fields and the funding-rate interval. ticker.percentage units and missing ccxt
fields on fetchPositions / fetchMyTrades rows were reported separately by others and are not claimed
here.

 Evidence

Re-run 2026-10-29 14:40 UTC, head block 36316343 (same shape as 2026-10-22 04:38/04:39 UTC):

 fetchMarkets()["ETH/USDso:USDso"] keys:
 id,chainId,symbol,type,base,quote,settle,active,contract,precision,limits,indexed,perpStatus,info
   ccxt unified keys missing: spot,swap,future,option,margin,linear,inverse,contractSize,taker,maker
   typical ccxt notional = amount * market.contractSize * price -> NaN
 perp markets: 21; with contractSize: 0; with linear: 0
 fetchTicker.fundingRate=0.0001; per-interval rate (fundingIntervalSec=3600) = 0.0000125; ticker value is
 the per-8h rate
 fetchFundingRateHistory rows fundingRate=0.0001,0.0001 (also per-8h; settlement every 3600s)


Raw: evidence/reverify-2026-10-29/s22.log. Sources: https://unpkg.com/@somnia-chain/markets-
sdk@0.32.1/docs/EXCHANGE.md, https://unpkg.com/@somnia-chain/markets-sdk@0.32.1/dist/unified/
structs.d.ts

 Suggested fix

For perps add contractSize: 1, linear: true, inverse: false, swap: true, spot: false and maker/taker;
put the per-interval rate in fundingRate with interval: "1h" and keep the 8h figure under its own name.
At minimum, list these differences in EXCHANGE.md.

 Environment

@somnia-chain/markets-sdk 0.32.1, Somnia Tokyo testnet (Hideki, chain 50383), wallet: none (read-
only)
