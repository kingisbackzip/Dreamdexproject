WS Operations getMarkets example: its symbol filter is silently ignored, and the example response shows quoteDecimals: 6 and tick/lot/min sizes that match no live market

Severity: normal
Area: docs

 Steps to reproduce

   1. Read https://app.testnet.dreamdex.io/docs/developers/websocket-api/operations. Request
      example: {"id":1,"operation":"getMarkets","params":{"symbol":"SOMI:USDso"},...} . The
      successResponse shows one SOMI:USDso market with "quoteDecimals": 6 , "tickSize": "0.00001" ,
      "lotSize": "0.000001" , "minQuantity": "0.001" .
   2. Connect to wss://stg.api.dreamdex.io/v0/ws/public and send
      {"id":2,"operation":"getMarkets","params":{"symbol":"SOMI:USDso"}} .
   3. Compare with curl https://stg.api.dreamdex.io/v0/markets and the Contract Addresses page
      (USDso = 18 decimals).

 Expected

The example request uses a parameter the operation honours, and the example response matches the
real values.

 Actual

    • params.symbol is ignored: the filtered request returns all 3 testnet markets (SOMI, WBTC, WETH),
      identical to the unfiltered getMarkets .
    • Every quote is USDso with 18 decimals (live quoteDecimals: 18 ), but the example shows
       quoteDecimals: 6 . Anyone who copies it mis-scales quote amounts by 1e12.
    • The example tick/lot/min values ( 0.00001 / 0.000001 / 0.001 ) match no live market; live
      SOMI:USDso is tickSize "0.0001" , lotSize "0.01" , minQuantity "1" .

Scope: the getMarkets example only. The missing kind / stopRegistry fields in typed market structs were
reported separately by others and are not claimed here.

 Evidence

Re-verified 2026-10-29 14:39 UTC (docs unchanged since 2026-10-20):

Raw: evidence/reverify-2026-10-29/ws-errors-run.txt, rest-markets.json

 Suggested fix

Drop params.symbol from the example (or implement the filter), and regenerate the example response
from a live SOMI:USDso row.

 Environment

WS/HTTP testnet endpoints the docs document: wss://stg.api.dreamdex.io/v0/ws/public and https://
stg.api.dreamdex.io/v0 (Somnia Shannon 50312). Wallet: none (read-only)
